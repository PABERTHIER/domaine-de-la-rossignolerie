# domaine-de-la-rossignolerie

Website project for Domaine de la Rossignolerie, a wine estate in Chalonnes-sur-Loire, Anjou.
French is the source language, with English for the same visitor journeys.
Preserve the estate's bird mark, vineyard and cellar photography, wine identities and personal voice.

## Project Scope

| Area               | Purpose                                                                         |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Estate             | Introduce the place, its story and the people behind the wines                  |
| Wines              | Present the six wine identities with their distinct vintages and supplied facts |
| Contact and visits | Make practical information and genuine enquiry actions easy to find             |
| Gallery            | Present the estate's photo projects in their supplied order                     |

Keep the recognizable presentation while improving readability, accessibility and mobile use within the requested scope.
An enquiry is distinct from a reservation or purchase.
Business details and wine information require factual review before publication.
